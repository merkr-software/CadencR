Do not edit `routeTree.gen.ts` — it is auto-generated by TanStack Router from the file-based routes.

A page the first screen never shows (Settings, Schedules, the Marketplace, the agents grid) keeps its component in `<route>.lazy.tsx` (`createLazyFileRoute`); `<route>.tsx` keeps only what the router needs before loading it (`validateSearch`, `beforeLoad`). Import nothing heavy into the non-lazy half: it ships in the entry chunk every phone downloads.
